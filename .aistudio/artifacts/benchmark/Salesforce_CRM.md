## 2. JUMO UEOS Platform Expansion Plan
Based on the Salesforce extraction, the JUMO UEOS platform kernel will expand to support:

### A. JUMO Relationship Manager (JRM)
- **Unified Profile (Customer 360)**: A single view of an entity (Student, Patient, Member, Vendor) across all ERP domains.
- **Interaction History**: Centralized log of all communications and transactions.

### B. JUMO Automation Engine (JAE)
- **Workflow Orchestrator**: A visual-like logic engine for cross-domain triggers (e.g., "If Student pays fee in DIGITAL PAY -> Update Status in Education ERP -> Post to FAAP").

### C. JUMO AI (Einstein-like)
- **Cognitive Assistant Registry**: Pre-configured AI roles for Sales, Service, and Audit.

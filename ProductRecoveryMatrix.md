## Recovery Traceability details

### 1. JUMO Fintech Expansion

- **Core Banking**: Digital Banking, Savings, Digital Wallets, Agent Banking.
- **Payments**: Universal Switch, Mobile Money, Gateway, Collections, Payouts, Remittances.
- **Lending**: Credit Underwriting, Microfinance JLG, SACCO, Embedded Finance.
- **Markets**: Treasury, FX Dealing, Investment, Insurance, Trade Finance.
- **Infrastructure**: Stablecoin Rails, Developer API, Compliance/AML, Data Intelligence.

### 2. Education ERP Consolidation
Unified Nursery and Primary into a single high-density ERP with shared Finance/Bursar and Admissions, while preserving sovereign academic modules:
- **ECD/Nursery**: Milestone Tracking, Play-based Academic logs.
- **Primary**: P.1-P.7 Academics, National PLE Candidate management.
- **Secondary**: O/A Level Subject Combinations, UNEB Center, Science Labs, Boarding.

### 3. Church & Alumni Institutional Recovery
Full restoration of ecclesiastical and advancement structures:
- **Church**: Diocesan Synod Hub, Parish Registers, Sacramental certificates, Tithe Stewardship.
- **Alumni**: Graduate Census directory, Global Chapters, Endowment campaigns, Mentorship pairing.

### 4. Global Capability Reintegration
Exposed 60+ executable capabilities across the ecosystem, mapped directly to:
- **AI Workforces**: Independent agent swarms per module.
- **Operational Forms**: Schema-driven digital forms (FAAP Journal, Student Registration, Member Census).
- **Horizontal Navigation**: Dynamic registry-driven horizontal navigation bar in every sovereign shell.

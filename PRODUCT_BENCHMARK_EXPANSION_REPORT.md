# PRODUCT BENCHMARK EXPANSION REPORT
## JUMO Universal Enterprise Operating System (UEOS) — Standalone Product Expansion Synthesis

This report presents the complete capability expansion across all 7 approved standalone JUMO products resulting from Depth-2 benchmark extraction.

---

### Product 1: JUMO FAAP (Financial & Accounting Platform)
* **Product Code**: `PROD_FAAP`
* **Ring Classification**: Ring-0 Financial Backbone & Settlement Engine
* **Standalone Capabilities Expanded**:
  - **Vote Book Encumbrance Engine**: Real-time commitment checking preventing over-commitment of department budget allocations.
  - **IPSAS 23 / IFRS 15 Grant Accounting**: Automated revenue recognition based on verified milestone completions.
  - **Multi-Fund Consolidation**: Automated elimination entries for inter-fund transfers across donor accounts.
  - **Benford's Law Fraud Detection**: Automated statistical audit checking for abnormal numeric frequency distributions in manual journal entries.
  - **Mobile Field Expense Vouchers**: Offline voucher creation with digital signatures queued in `offlineSyncService`.

---

### Product 2: JUMO Digital Pay (Payment & Switch Platform)
* **Product Code**: `PROD_DP`
* **Ring Classification**: Omni-Channel Payment Switch & Settlement Engine
* **Standalone Capabilities Expanded**:
  - **Dynamic Payment Rail Health Router**: Monitored failover routing across MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Bank EFT, and Visa/Mastercard.
  - **Atomic Multi-Split Settlement Engine**: Instant split payment routing executing 1.5% JUMO Master Treasury debit, 0.5% switch provider fee, and 98% merchant credit.
  - **SchoolPay 10-Digit Ingress Gateway**: Student fee code validation engine linking directly to educational institution ledgers.
  - **AI Fraud Radar**: Device fingerprinting, velocity checking, and risk score calculation (0-100).
  - **Offline Agent POS Mode**: Encrypted local transaction logging for field agents in remote locations.

### Product 3: JUMO Primary & Nursery ERP

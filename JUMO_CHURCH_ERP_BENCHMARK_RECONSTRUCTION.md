# JUMO Church ERP Benchmark Reconstruction

## 1. Executive Summary
JUMO CHURCH ERP is an independent, sovereign ecclesiastical platform designed for dioceses, synods, archdeaconries, and parish networks.

---

## 2. Ecclesiastical Modules & Registers
1. **Diocesan Synod & Bishop Office**:
   - Diocesan Synod resolutions, episcopal calendar, deanery stations, and archdeaconries.

2. **Parish & Curate Stations**:
   - Canonical parish stations, vicar/curate assignments, communicant directories, and station health metrics.

3. **Canonical Sacramental Registers**:
   - Holy Baptism, Confirmation, Holy Matrimony, and Holy Orders register books with verified reference numbering and digital certificates.

4. **Stewardship, Tithes & Diocesan Quota**:
   - Double-entry accounting for tithes, offerings, pledges, and missionary outreach funds directly posting to the FAAP general ledger.
   - Diocesan quota compliance tracking across constituent parishes.

5. **Church Control & Developer Centers**:
   - Ecclesiastical parameters, lectionary schedules, and REST APIs for giving and membership synchronization.

## BRANDING GUIDELINES
1. **Source Code**: All original license headers and copyright notices MUST remain intact.
2. **User Interface**: Jumo will overlay its brand (Logo, Colors, Name) where the license permits (e.g., product names in the UI, CSS overrides).
3. **Attribution**: A "Powered by [Application Name]" or "Powered by Jumo" footer will be maintained as required by the respective licenses.
4. **Trademarks**: Jumo will respect all third-party trademarks (e.g., "ERPNext" is a trademark of Frappe Technologies). We will use "Jumo ERP (Powered by ERPNext)" or similar nomenclature.

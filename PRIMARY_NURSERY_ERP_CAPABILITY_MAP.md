# JUMO Primary & Nursery ERP Capability Map

## 1. Domain Specification
JUMO Primary & Nursery ERP (`PROD_EDU_PRIMARY`, Template: `TMPL_EDU_PRE_PRIMARY`) is benchmarked against Hillside Nalya Primary School. It provides early childhood, nursery, kindergarten, and primary school management.

---

## 2. Core Functional Modules

### A. Nursery Welfare & Early Childhood Development (`EDU_MOD_NURSERY`)
- **Milestone Tracking**: Motor skills, language acquisition, social interaction, and cognitive development logs.
- **Daily Welfare Logs**: Nap times, meal consumption, potty training logs, and medication administration notes.
- **Parent Daily Updates**: Automated digital daily diary pushed to parent mobile apps at the end of each school day.

### B. Pupil Lifecycle & Family Management (`EDU_MOD_PUPILS`)
- **Pupil Profiles**: Admission numbers, passport photos, immunization records, emergency contacts, and allergy alerts.
- **Parent / Guardian Linkage**: Links multiple siblings to a single parent account for unified fee billing and messaging.

### C. Primary Academic Assessment & Report Cards (`EDU_MOD_ASSESS`)
- **Termly Testing Cycles**: Beginning of Term (BOT), Mid-Term (MOT), and End of Term (EOT) continuous assessment marks.
- **Automated Report Cards**: Generates printable report cards with subject marks, class averages, teacher remarks, headteacher comments, and attendance records.

### D. Pupil Transport & Fleet Safety (`EDU_MOD_TRANSPORT`)
- **Route Allocation**: Assigns pupils to school van/bus routes and designated pickup/drop-off points.
- **Driver & Matron Logs**: Real-time driver identification, matron attendance logs, and pupil boarding/alighting timestamps.

### E. Primary Canteen & Meal Management
- **Dietary Restriction Records**: Tracks vegetarian, halal, or allergy-specific meal plans.
- **Meal Voucher Tokens**: Digital canteen voucher system integrated with parent mobile top-up accounts.

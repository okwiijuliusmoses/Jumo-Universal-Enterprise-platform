# JUMO Church & Diocese ERP Capability Map

## 1. Domain Specification
JUMO Church & Diocese ERP (`PROD_CH`) provides faith-based organizational management across the ecclesiastical hierarchy (Province -> Diocese -> Archdeaconry -> Parish -> Local Church).

---

## 2. Core Functional Modules

### A. Diocesan Structure & Episcopal Governance (`CH_MOD_DIOCESE`)
- **Hierarchical Jurisdiction**: Manages multi-tiered diocesan administrative structures, bishop decrees, and diocesan synod resolutions.
- **Diocesan Quota Assessments**: Calculates annual parish quota contributions based on parish membership size and revenue baseline.

### B. Sacramental Register Books (`CH_MOD_SACRAMENT`)
- **Holy Baptism Register**: Canonical baptism records (child name, date of birth, parents, godparents, officiating priest, baptism certificate number).
- **Confirmation Register**: Confirmation records with bishop signature logs and confirmation card generation.
- **Holy Matrimony Register**: Banns of marriage publication records, marriage license numbers, bride/groom details, and canonical certificate registers.

### C. Stewardship, Tithes & Building Pledges (`CH_MOD_TITHE`)
- **Member Tithe Accounts**: Envelope tracking, digital mobile tithe payments via JUMO Digital Pay, and termly stewardship statement issuance.
- **Building Fund & Special Pledges**: Multi-year pledge commitment tracking, installment payment logging, and pledge completion certificates.

### D. Clergy & Pastoral Care Administration (`CH_MOD_CLERGY`)
- **Clergy Deployment History**: Ordination dates, licensing records, parish posting timelines, and clergy stipend disbursement schedules.
- **Pastoral Care Logs**: Sick communion visits, bereavement counselling records, and home fellowship reports.

# JUMO UEOS Alumni ERP Architecture Rules

## 1. Education ERP Alumni Components

Alumni functionality inside education ERPs is NOT a standalone ERP.

Examples:

University ERP:
- Alumni & Advancement Portal
- Graduate relations
- Tracer studies
- Graduation engagement
- Endowment relationship management

College ERP:
- Alumni Secretariat Portal
- Graduate registry
- Alumni communication
- College advancement

Secondary ERP:
- Old Students Association Portal
- Former student registry
- Reunion management

These remain subordinate modules/components of their parent Education ERP.

---

## 2. Standalone Alumni Network ERP

Alumni Network ERP is an independent JUMO ERP family.

It has independent governance, portals, workflows and modules.

Core portals:

- Alumni Executive Office Portal
- Alumni Membership & Directory Portal
- Global & Regional Chapters Portal
- Career Development & Mentorship Portal
- Alumni Advancement & Outreach Portal
- Capital Campaigns & Fundraising Portal
- Alumni Endowment Fund & FAAP Treasury Portal
- Alumni Enterprise & Business Network Portal
- Alumni Investment & Venture Network Portal
- Alumni Financial Services & SACCO Portal
- Alumni Communications Portal
- Global Alumni Events Portal
- Alumni Research & Analytics Portal
- Alumni Digital Identity Services Portal
- Alumni Compliance & AEGIS Audit Portal

---

## 3. Isolation Rule

Standalone Alumni ERP must never contain:

- University governance
- Senate
- Council
- Registrar
- Faculties
- Academic departments
- Student management
- Academic workflows

Those belong only to Education ERP families.

---

## 4. JUMO UEOS Implementation Principle

Every ERP family must have:

- Independent governance architecture
- Independent portals
- Independent modules
- Independent workflows
- Independent terminology
- Shared UEOS enterprise framework only

No ERP family should inherit another ERP's institutional structure.

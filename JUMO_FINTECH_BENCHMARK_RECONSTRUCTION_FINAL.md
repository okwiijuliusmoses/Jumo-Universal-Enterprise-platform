# JUMO Fintech Benchmark Reconstruction Final

## 1. Executive Summary
JUMO FINTECH is the sovereign financial operating platform of JUMO UEOS, incorporating the Financial & Accounting Platform (FAAP) core and the 42 specialized financial family modules.

---

## 2. Benchmark Architecture & Core Capabilities
1. **FAAP General Ledger**:
   - Double-entry accounting system with automated balance checks ($0.00 offset).
   - Real-time Chart of Accounts, General Journal, Accounts Payable, and Accounts Receivable.
   - Bank reconciliation and direct statement import feeds.

2. **Master Treasury & Settlement Router**:
   - Global 1.5% settlement clearing fee automation.
   - Instantaneous inter-account debit/credit routing.
   - Multi-currency Foreign Exchange (FX) with real-time rate feeds.

3. **Digital Pay & Financial Families**:
   - Mobile Money gateways, Merchant Acquiring, Virtual and Physical Card issuance.
   - Agent Banking network with liquidity rebalancing and float management.
   - Microfinance and SACCO loan management, credit scoring, and collateral tracking.

4. **Sovereign Administration & Developer Portals**:
   - Dedicated Fintech Control Center for institution liquidity rules and clearing limits.
   - Dedicated Fintech Developer Center for Open Banking REST APIs and Webhook endpoints.

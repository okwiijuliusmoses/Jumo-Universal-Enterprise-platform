# JUMO UEOS Product Inventory

## 1. Product Family Overview
JUMO Universal Enterprise Operating System (UEOS) organizes all software capabilities into a family of standalone, registry-driven enterprise products. Each product operates under its own distinct branding, information architecture, navigation model, and workspace shell while consuming shared platform services (Identity, FAAP Ledger, Digital Pay Switch, Cognitive AI, and Security).

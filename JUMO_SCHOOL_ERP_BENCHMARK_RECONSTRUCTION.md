# JUMO Universal School ERP Benchmark Reconstruction

## 1. Executive Summary
JUMO UNIVERSAL SCHOOL ERP has been reconstructed as an independent, sovereign educational operating environment. The application features a clean white enterprise visual design and is powered by the `TemplateRegistry.ts` engine.

---

## 2. Institutional Tiers & Capability Manifests
The application dynamically configures its office portals, workflows, and curriculum terminology based on the active institutional tier:
1. **Pre-Primary / Kindergarten (ECCE)**: Developmental milestones, caregiver rosters, early childhood nutrition.
2. **Primary School (P.1 – P.7)**: Thematic curriculum, continuous assessment, PLE examination indexation.
3. **Secondary School (S.1 – S.6)**: O/A Level combination registers, NCDC competency grading, UNEB Center (UCE/UACE).
4. **Tertiary & University ERP**: Senate assemblies, academic faculties, course unit transcripts, hostel warden registers.
5. **Vocational & TVET Institute**: CBET trade assessments, workshop safety registers, industrial attachment logs.

---

## 3. Dedicated Office Workspaces (Non-Card Layouts)
The product replaces card clusters with real operational registers:
- **Bursar Office Portal**: Alpha Cash Book, Fees Ledgers, Payment Vouchers, Bank & PRN Reconciliation feeds.
- **Registrar Office Portal**: Admissions intake, Master Student Directory (SIS), Stream capacity balancing, End-of-year promotion engines.
- **Academic Office (DOS) Portal**: Assessment marks entry, Activity of Integration (AOI) scoring, Terminal report card compiler, Master timetable grid, UNEB Candidate Center.
- **Head Teacher Executive Portal**: Board of Governors resolutions, Teaching and Administrative staff appraisal roster, MoES statutory compliance.
- **Teacher & Gradebook Portal**: Daily roll call registers, continuous assessment scoring sheets, lesson plans.
- **Parent & Student Portal**: Terminal report cards, fee payment statements, and live PRN payment tokens.
- **School ERP Control Center**: Institutional parameters, academic calendar, and tier activation.
- **School ERP Developer Center**: REST endpoints, EMIS synchronization, and webhook subscriptions.

# Benchmark Extraction: Alpha Academy
## Level 1 — Product Identity
*   **Product Name:** Alpha Academy School ERP
*   **Product Category:** K-12 School Management System
*   **Target Market:** Nursery, Primary, and Secondary educational systems.
*   **Target Organizations:** Private schools, religious educational groups, and municipal public school networks.
*   **Target Users:** Parents, Teachers, School Administrators, Bursars, and Students.
*   **Deployment Model:** Cloud SaaS with responsive mobile-web views.
*   **Major Product Editions:** K-12 Standard, Academy Pro (Multi-Branch).

---

## Level 2 — Organizational Structure & Offices
*   **Headteacher/Principal Desk:** Core operational oversight and general assembly directives.
*   **Academic Office (Director of Studies):** Coordinates class rosters, curriculum syllabus progress, termly report cards, and homework logs.
*   **Bursar Office:** Handles cash collections, termly school fees invoices, feeding programs, and transport fees.
*   **Boarding & Dormitory Office:** Tracks dorm bed allocations, visitation days, and boarding requirements.
*   **Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) Liaison:** Coordinates communications, newsletters, and SMS notifications.

---

## Level 3 — Core Functional Modules
*   **Student Admissions Module:** Simple intake form collecting parent information, medical alerts, and transport needs.
*   **Termly Report Card Module:** Computes grades, teacher comments, and headteacher signatures for end-of-term distributions.
*   **Transport Routing & Roster Module:** Manages bus assignments, driver logs, and route pick-up points.
*   **SMS Broadcasting Utility:** Allows instant alert delivery to parent contacts regarding school fees deadlines or emergency holidays.

---

## Level 4 — Portals and Access Levels
*   **Parent Portal:** View child demographics, term report card results, active fee statements, and authorize transport enrollments.
*   **Teacher Portal:** Log daily attendance registers, insert term marks, and post behavioral comments.
*   **Administrator Portal:** System controls, staff rosters, fee collection summaries, and system settings.

---

## Level 5 — Core Business Workflows
1.  **Student Intake & Parent Linkage:**
    *   *Trigger:* New student registers.
    *   *Input:* Student name, parent email, parent phone.
    *   *Outcome:* Automatically generates student profile and links to parent portal credentials.
2.  **Term Fee Invoicing & Collection:**
    *   *Trigger:* Termly billing cycle runs.
    *   *Billing:* Standardized tuition invoice distributed to linked parents.
    *   *Downstream:* Integrated with schoolpay payment gateways to reconcile payment reference numbers (PRN).

# Benchmark Extraction: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance
## Level 1 — Product Identity
*   **Product Name:** Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance
*   **Product Category:** Mid-Market and Enterprise Financial Management System
*   **Target Market:** Enterprise organizations, manufacturing industries, and global retail chains.
*   **Target Users:** CFOs, corporate controllers, international tax coordinators.
*   **Deployment Model:** Azure Cloud SaaS.
*   **API/Developer Surfaces:** OData Services, custom Data Management Framework (DIXF).

---

## Level 2 — Organizational Structure & Offices
*   **Global Tax Control:** Configures localized regulatory tax structures (e.g., VAT, Sales Tax) globally.
*   **Treasury & Cash Management:** Controls bank statement reconciliations, cash-flow forecasts, and cash pool management.

---

## Level 3 — Core Functional Modules
*   **General Ledger Engine:** Supports multi-company consolidation, multi-currency conversions, and custom financial dimensions.
*   **Budgeting & Control Core:** Custom budget planning worksheets with real-time enforcement checks on transactions.
*   **Asset Management Module:** Fully integrated tracker managing asset acquisition, depreciation, write-downs, and disposals.

---

## Level 4 — Portals and Access Levels
*   **Finance Manager Dashboard:** Displays financial indicators (ROI, Profit Margins), budget variances, and cash forecasts.
*   **Audit Trail Workspace:** Searchable database logs mapping transaction changes, creation dates, and user IDs.

---

## Level 5 — Core Business Workflows
1.  **Budget Control Enforcement:**
    *   *Trigger:* Purchase order is drafted.
    *   *Check:* System verifies budget head availability.
    *   *Overdraft:* If purchase exceeds remaining budget, holds order and requests approval.
2.  **Multinational Financial Consolidation:**
    *   *Trigger:* End of fiscal quarter.
    *   *Consolidation:* Maps subsidiary chart of accounts to parent ledger and calculates consolidation sheets.

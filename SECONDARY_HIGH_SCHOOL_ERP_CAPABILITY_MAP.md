# JUMO Secondary & High School ERP Capability Map

## 1. Domain Specification
JUMO Secondary & High School ERP (`PROD_EDU_SECONDARY`, Template: `TMPL_EDU_HIGH_SCHOOL`) is benchmarked against Alpha Academy. It provides comprehensive O-Level and A-Level high school management.

---

## 2. Core Functional Modules

### A. O-Level & A-Level Academic Administration (`EDU_MOD_COMBOS`)
- **Subject Combination Builder**: Manages A-Level principal subject combinations (PCM/M, BCM/Sub-Math, HEG/ICT, MEG/Art, GEA/Lit) and O-Level compulsory/elective subjects.
- **Academic Stream Allocation**: Organizes large student bodies into streams (e.g., Senior 1 Red, Blue, Green, White).

### B. National Examination Candidate Processing (`EDU_MOD_UNEB`)
- **Candidate Registration**: Prepares UNEB/National examination candidate files, index number verification, and passport photo uploads.
- **Mock Exam Ranking & Item Analysis**: Tracks mock examination performance, item difficulty analysis, and subject failure risk flags.

### C. Science Laboratory & Equipment Tracker (`EDU_MOD_LABS`)
- **Apparatus & Reagents Inventory**: Chemistry, Biology, and Physics laboratory equipment tracking, chemical reorder thresholds, and breakage registers.
- **Practical Session Scheduling**: Science lab timetable allocation for A-Level and O-Level practical sessions.

### D. Boarding & Dormitory System (`EDU_MOD_DORMS`)
- **House & Dormitory Allocation**: Assigns boarders to houses/dormitories and specific bed numbers.
- **Housemaster & Senior Woman Teacher Logs**: Inspection logs, roll call attendance, roll-call exemption passes, and health clinic referrals.

### E. Student Leadership & Prefects Council
- **Prefect Election System**: Digital voting for Head Prefect, Academic Prefect, Dormitory Prefects, and Canteen Prefects.
- **Disciplinary Council Records**: Disciplinary committee case files, sanction tracking, and parent suspension notifications.
